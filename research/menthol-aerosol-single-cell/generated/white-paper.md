## Next research steps

**Proposed:** A discriminating follow-up would randomize independent mice across collection days and compare vehicle, nicotine-only, purified menthol, menthol plus nicotine, and matched aerosol controls within each sex. Analyze validated cell types using mouse-level pseudobulk and include measured aerosol chemistry and exposure dose. (claim C08; [1](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE263903), [2](https://elifesciences.org/articles/106380))
