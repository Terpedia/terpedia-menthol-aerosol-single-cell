# Critical review: GSE263903 menthol-mint aerosol single-cell audit

The [GEO series](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE263903) supplies six useful 10x count matrices for a compact analysis of air, PG:VG, and menthol-mint aerosol arms. It does **not** supply an isolated menthol intervention. The [published study](https://elifesciences.org/articles/106380) reports tobacco-derived nicotine in the flavored e-liquids, flavor-dependent aerosol metals, and pooling of two mice per sex and condition before each single-cell library. The scRNA-seq dataset has one male and one female library per condition, and GEO's collection dates differ by condition. [eLife's assessment](https://elifesciences.org/articles/106380#assessment) also identifies limited biological replication.

This notebook uses already filtered 10x matrices and applies its own basic cell thresholds. Its five-gene marker panels produce **marker-enriched bins**, not validated cell types. The apparent myeloid-bin fractions can change through cell capture, QC, subtype mixture, and marker expression. They cannot be read as mouse-level cell proportions. The lower cell yield in the male PG:VG library (2,451 passing cells) makes composition comparisons especially fragile.

Within the myeloid-marker bin, pooled-library `Il1b` and `Cxcl2` log2 CPM are higher in both menthol-mint libraries than in their sex-matched PG:VG libraries. The female differences are only +0.19 and +0.21 against air, versus +1.46 and +1.19 against PG:VG. This baseline sensitivity means the available data do not support a stable effect size for a specific chemical. Neither pooled cells nor the two sex-labeled libraries justify a differential-expression p-value, a claim of biological replication, or target attribution.

The targeted metabolic panel offers no shared `Hmgcr` direction in the two menthol-mint versus PG:VG sex-pool comparisons (+0.07 male, -0.13 female log2 CPM). That is a narrow observation about one gene, not evidence that cholesterol synthesis is unaffected. `Trpm8` has very low counts in these marker bins, so pseudocount-driven log ratios are not target-engagement evidence.

The next experiment should include independent mouse libraries randomized across collection dates, sex balance, measured aerosol chemistry, and controls for vehicle, nicotine, purified menthol, menthol plus nicotine, and aerosolization. Cell labels should be validated before mouse-level pseudobulk analysis. The current project remains a useful demonstration of Terputer's input hashing, fresh-kernel notebook execution, claim ledger, and restrained interpretation.
